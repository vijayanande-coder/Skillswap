Student Marketplace Model

One Platform. Three Genuine Opportunities.

Learn new skills for free with peer barter, or earn real income while in college.

1. Free Skill Exchange

Trade skills directly with other students without any cash involved. You teach Python or UI design, they teach you conversational language or calculus.

  • Zero monetary fee or commission
  • Mutual learning agreements
  • Expand knowledge without a budget

2. Student Freelancing

Hire talented students for frontend coding, logo design, video editing, or resume polish at transparent rates in Indian Rupees (₹).

  • Clear pricing in ₹ (INR)
  • Build your real-world portfolio
  • Earn money while studying

3. 1-on-1 Peer Tutoring

Get personalized academic tutoring and interview prep from university upperclassmen who recently aced your exact subjects.

  • Hourly video sessions in ₹/hr
  • Calculus, CS, Physics & Engineering
  • Flexible scheduling around classes

How SkillSwap Works

Get started in 3 simple steps.

1

Register Your Student Profile

Sign up with your college email. Add the skills you can offer (e.g. coding, design) and what you want to learn.

2

Publish a Service or Propose a Barter

List a free skill exchange or a paid freelance gig in Indian Rupees (₹). Or propose a mutual exchange on an existing listing.

3

Collaborate & Track in Dashboard

Manage incoming requests and bookings inside your student dashboard. Complete work, build experience, and track your simulated earnings.
